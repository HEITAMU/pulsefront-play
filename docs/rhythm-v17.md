# v1.7.0 — first-ten-stage rhythm trial

Stages 1–10 now use a shared beat score for enemy entries, side warnings and musical tom lead-ins. Later encounters retain the previous campaign schedule. Small enemies pulse forward; chargers compress for two beats before dashing; heavy targets resist waves and take extra laser damage. The first boss cues periodic charges. Hit suppression has a per-enemy rate limit and heavy resistance.

Hold the main button and swipe up 34 CSS pixels within 650 ms to change weapon. Side buttons and keyboard 1/2 remain. Changes are immediate, preserve held fire, and cannot fire after clearing. An on-beat switch within 125 ms produces an amplified opening shot, at most once per four beats. Clicks, sway and switching share beat identity, so one beat cannot be farmed across input methods.

Energy enemies grant six seconds of armor bypass, stronger suppression or extra support volleys. Firepower Workshop adds capped permanent power, rate and support upgrades; actual kill salvage is retained on defeat. Existing saves normalize to level-zero upgrades. No paid currency, vibration or real ad monetization added.

Validation: full preexisting regression suite remains explicitly on legacy encounters; 120 new trial runs cover 10 stages, two phone dimensions, three seeds and two strategies. Introductory stages remain accessible with hold. At higher trial stages unattended hold takes damage and can lose, while deliberate switching every four beats plus alternate-beat gestures/aim can clear without purchased upgrades. These are simulation checks, not proof of fun or physical-phone audio latency.

Prior final-six-second taper, final-two-second spawn cutoff, music fade and cleared-field tail remain.
